COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Code of Conduct

Dinner, Dice & Dragons exists to help people enjoy tabletop RPGs together in welcoming public spaces. Participation requires respect for other people, the table, and the venue.

Our baseline

Everyone deserves to participate without harassment, intimidation, unwanted sexual conduct, threats, discrimination, theft, or deliberate disruption.

Players, Game Masters, venue staff, guests, and moderators are all covered by these standards.

At every table

  • Respect stated table boundaries and content expectations.
  • Respect a person’s name, identity, physical space, and right to disengage.
  • Do not pressure someone to participate in content they have declined.
  • Follow the host venue’s lawful policies.
  • Do not bring weapons, illegal drugs, or dangerous conduct into an event.
  • Alcohol use never excuses disruptive, threatening, or harassing behavior.

FOR PLAYERS

Be the kind of person people want back at the table.

  • Show up for seats you confirm, or cancel as early as reasonably possible.
  • Do not repeatedly interrupt, bully, insult, or deliberately sabotage other participants.
  • Separate in-character conflict from real-world hostility.
  • Follow the GM’s published table expectations and the venue’s rules.
  • Raise safety concerns without retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

FOR GAME MASTERS

Set expectations clearly and run the table fairly.

  • Accurately describe the game’s system, tone, content, experience level, and expected duration.
  • Make table expectations available before a player commits.
  • Do not use the GM role to pressure players into unwanted personal, romantic, sexual, financial, or off-platform interactions.
  • Respond reasonably when a player invokes an agreed table boundary or safety tool.
  • Record attendance truthfully and do not weaponize feedback tools.

FOR VENUES

Provide a clear, public place to meet.

  • Provide accurate information about availability, age restrictions, accessibility, and purchase requirements.
  • Treat tabletop groups under the same lawful customer standards as other patrons.
  • Notify organizers promptly when a confirmed reservation can no longer be honored.
  • Venue staff are not expected to moderate game rules, but normal venue safety policies still apply.

REPORTING

Reports are reviewed; they are not automatic verdicts.

Participants should be able to privately report harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual behavior, theft, severe disruption, repeated no-shows, or boundary violations.

Possible responses

  • Request for additional information
  • Warning or education
  • Loss of instant-join or hosting privileges
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent removal for severe or repeated misconduct
  • Referral to venue staff or emergency services when appropriate

Moderation decisions should consider evidence, severity, context, and patterns of independent reports.

Agreement is required to participate.

Player, Game Master, and partner venue onboarding will require affirmative acceptance of this Code of Conduct.
