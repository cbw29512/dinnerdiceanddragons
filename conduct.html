SAFE TABLES ARE VIABLE TABLES

Clear expectations before strangers meet.

Matching people by game and distance is not enough. Dinner, Dice & Dragons uses public venues, table expectations, commitment history, structured feedback, and private reporting to reduce avoidable risk.

Our baseline

Everyone deserves to participate without harassment, intimidation, unwanted sexual conduct, threats, discrimination, theft, or deliberate disruption.

Players, GMs, venue staff, guests, and moderators are covered by these standards.

At every table

  • Respect published table boundaries and content expectations.
  • Respect names, identities, physical space, and the right to disengage.
  • Do not pressure someone into declined content or unwanted personal interaction.
  • Follow the public venue’s lawful policies.
  • Do not bring weapons, illegal drugs, or dangerous conduct into an event.
  • Alcohol never excuses disruptive, threatening, or harassing behavior.

BETTER THAN A SINGLE STAR SCORE

Trust should answer useful questions.

A five-star popularity number can hide the information people actually need. We prefer structured, role-specific signals and a neutral New to DDD state for people without verified history.

🧙 GM trust

Games hosted · completed as scheduled · description accuracy · boundaries respected · would play again.

🎲 Player trust

Sessions joined · attendance rate · late cancellations/no-shows · table-respect feedback.

🍽️ Venue trust

Sessions hosted · accessibility information accuracy · table suitability · would return.

Self-reported years of experience remain separate from platform-observed reliability. See how reputation works.

FOR PLAYERS

A confirmed seat is a commitment.

  • Show up for seats you confirm, or cancel as early as reasonably possible.
  • Do not repeatedly interrupt, bully, insult, or deliberately sabotage others.
  • Separate in-character conflict from real-world hostility.
  • Follow the GM’s published expectations and venue rules.
  • Do not retaliate against someone for raising a safety concern.

FOR GAME MASTERS

Accurate listings prevent bad matches.

  • Accurately describe system, tone, content, experience level, duration, and table style.
  • Publish important boundaries before Players commit.
  • Do not use the GM role to pressure Players into unwanted personal, romantic, sexual, financial, or off-platform interaction.
  • Respond reasonably to agreed safety tools and boundaries.
  • Record attendance truthfully and never weaponize feedback.

FOR VENUES

A predictable public environment is part of Table Fit.

  • Provide accurate availability, age/alcohol, accessibility, seating, noise, and purchase information.
  • Treat tabletop groups under the same lawful customer standards as other patrons.
  • Notify the GM promptly if a confirmed table can no longer be honored.
  • Venue staff do not moderate RPG rules; normal venue safety policies still apply.

PRIVATE REPORTING

A report is evidence to review—not an automatic public penalty.

Participants should be able to privately report harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual behavior, theft, severe disruption, repeated no-shows, or boundary violations.

Possible responses

  • Request additional information
  • Warning or education
  • Loss of instant-join or hosting privileges
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent removal for severe or repeated misconduct
  • Referral to venue staff or emergency services when appropriate

Moderation should consider evidence, severity, context, and independent patterns.

Table Fit includes safety and environment.

The goal is not to promise that every human interaction will be perfect. The goal is to give people better information, meet in public places, set expectations early, and respond responsibly when something goes wrong.
