# Dinner, Dice & Dragons — Reputation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how verified platform history may be summarized publicly without turning reputation into a barrier for newcomers or a weapon against GMs, Players, or Venues.

## Core rule

**No history is neutral.**

A new Player, GM, or Venue is not treated as unreliable merely because Dinner, Dice & Dragons has not observed enough activity yet.

## Public history states

### New to DDD
Use when the platform lacks enough verified history for meaningful aggregates.

Show role, clearly labeled self-reported experience where relevant, and `No verified platform history yet`.

Do not show zero-star ratings, `0% reliability`, warning colors solely because history is missing, or negative match adjustments.

### Building History
Use when verified platform activity exists but sample thresholds are not yet met for some aggregates.

### Established
Use when enough eligible verified evidence exists to show meaningful aggregate signals.

### Caution
May be shown only when a published, evidence-based reliability threshold is met. It is not triggered by one complaint or one negative review.

## Initial sample-threshold proposal

For prototype/policy testing only:

- 0–4 eligible responses: do not publish a would-repeat percentage
- 5+ eligible responses: percentage may be shown with response count

The denominator must always be available to the UI.

## Matching policy

Reputation is not a hard constraint for ordinary Table Match.

Matching order:
1. required logistical constraints
2. table/preference compatibility
3. verified reliability context only after viable matches exist

A newcomer with excellent fit may rank above an established user with materially worse logistics or table fit.

## Attendance policy

Attendance records distinguish attended, excused absence, late cancellation, and no-show.

An excused absence does not count as a no-show. Do not reduce public reliability from a cancellation caused by the platform, venue, or GM.

## Feedback policy

Reputation-bearing feedback requires an eligible completed-event relationship.

One person may not submit multiple reputation-bearing responses about the same subject for the same event unless the product explicitly defines multiple independent dimensions in one response.

Free-text feedback is private by default.

## Venue feedback policy

Venue reputation is specifically about **hosting tabletop sessions through Dinner, Dice & Dragons**. It is not a substitute for Yelp, Google Reviews, or a general restaurant review system.

### Eligibility

A Player or GM may submit venue reputation-bearing feedback only when:
- the Event is completed,
- the venue hosted that Event,
- the person was an eligible confirmed participant/GM for that Event,
- the person has not already submitted venue feedback for that Event.

No anonymous, drive-by, pre-event, or unrelated customer review may affect venue reputation.

### Structured public dimensions

Public venue trust may aggregate:
- reserved table available as promised,
- table/space suitable for tabletop play,
- group treated as welcome customers,
- environment/noise description accurate,
- accessibility information accurate where applicable,
- venue-caused cancellation rate,
- would play here again / would return for tabletop play.

These dimensions are about the RPG-hosting relationship, not food quality, server tips, political opinions, unrelated disputes, or general business grievances.

### No public review wall

Dinner, Dice & Dragons must not publish an unrestricted chronological feed of individual venue comments.

Free-text venue feedback is private by default and may be used for:
- moderation/safety review,
- correcting inaccurate venue data,
- resolving a specific operational issue,
- providing private constructive feedback to a verified venue manager where policy permits.

It does not become a public quote merely because a user submitted it.

### Anti-review-bombing controls

- one reputation-bearing venue response per eligible person per completed Event,
- verified identity required,
- completed-event eligibility required,
- rate limits apply,
- duplicate/linked-account abuse may be investigated,
- public percentages require minimum sample thresholds,
- sudden anomalous feedback bursts may be held for review,
- a single negative response never creates a public caution state,
- moderation reports remain separate from public venue aggregates,
- venue managers may dispute demonstrably incorrect event/attendance source records.

### Venue fairness

A venue new to DDD displays `New to DDD` or `Building History`, never a zero rating.

A newly participating venue is fully eligible for Table Match based on logistics, capacity, policies, accessibility/environment fit, and verification status. Missing reputation history does not reduce its ordinary Table Fit score.

## Moderation separation

Reports and allegations do not automatically alter public reputation.

Moderators may apply account/venue restrictions based on safety policy and evidence. These restrictions are authorization/moderation state, not a popularity score.

## Appeals and correction

Production moderation/reputation systems must support correcting authoritative source records when attendance, event status, venue identity, or venue-hosting status is demonstrably wrong.

Derived reputation snapshots are recalculated from corrected evidence.

## Fair-start audit

Regularly compare newcomer and established-user/venue outcomes after controlling for eligibility and Table Fit.

Watch impressions per eligible match, invitations/requests per eligible match, confirmation rate, and played-session rate.

If new users or venues are systematically disadvantaged because of history state rather than fit, adjust ranking/presentation logic.
