NOT ANOTHER GROUP CHAT

We help the game actually happen.

Social platforms are good at letting people post, RSVP, and talk. Dinner, Dice & Dragons is designed around the harder part: turning scattered local interest into a viable table.

DEMAND WORKS BOTH WAYS

The platform should tell people where a table is most likely to form.

We are not limited to waiting for a GM to post an event. Player demand, GM availability, and venue capacity can all create opportunities.

🎲

Player demand

“5 nearby Players want Pathfinder Saturday.”

A compatible GM can see that demand and decide to run a game.

🧙

GM availability

“A D&D GM is free every other Tuesday.”

The system can show matching venues and nearby Players who want that system.

🍽️

Venue availability

“Two tables are open Wednesday 6–10 PM.”

Nearby GMs can claim a viable table window and publish a game.
