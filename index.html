HOW IT WORKS

Three signals. One real game night.

Any side can start. Dinner, Dice & Dragons looks for the overlap instead of forcing everyone through the same entry point.

1 · SIGNAL

Say what works for you

Players post demand. GMs post what they can run. Venues post table windows and capacity.

2 · TABLE MATCH

Check the hard fit first

System, schedule, travel distance, venue availability, capacity, and required table needs determine whether a match is viable.

3 · FORM → CONFIRM

Commit, then coordinate

A viable match becomes a Forming table. Once the venue and required Players commit, the confirmed group moves into the Game Hub.
