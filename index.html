HOW IT WORKS

Three needs. One game night.

The GM needs Players and a place. Players need a GM and a game. The venue wants a group at its tables. We find the overlap.

1

GM posts availability

The GM tells us the RPG system, when they can run, and how far they are willing to travel.

2

Players + venue match

We find Players who want that game and can make that time, plus public venues that can host the group.

3

GM forms the game

The GM approves the Players and venue. The venue confirms the table. Everyone moves into one Game Hub.
