TRUST & SAFETY

Good games start with clear expectations.

Public venues first, structured table expectations, attendance history, private reporting, and evidence-based trust signals help reduce surprises without turning people into star ratings.

Built into every table

  • Public or community-friendly venue details
  • Table culture and content expectations
  • Clear joining rules
  • Attendance and reliability signals
  • Private moderation workflow
