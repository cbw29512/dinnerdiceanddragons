HOW IT WORKS

The hard part is getting everyone to line up. That is what we solve.

You should not have to understand a matching algorithm. Tell us what works for you and we show you where the overlap is.

1

Tell us what works

Game, schedule, travel range, table needs, and venue availability.

2

We find the overlap

Table Match checks whether the Players, DM, place, time, and capacity can work together.

3

Form the table

The DM chooses the game and venue. Players request seats. The venue approves the booking when required.

4

Confirm and play

Once the required people and venue are confirmed, everyone gets one place for the game-night details.
