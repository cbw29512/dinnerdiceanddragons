HOW IT WORKS

Start from any side. We look for the other two.

There is no required starting point. Player interest, a GM offer, or a venue opening can reveal the same game-night opportunity.

🎲

Player-first

A Player posts the game and times they want. We look for compatible GMs and public venues.

🧙

GM-first

A GM posts what they want to run and when. We look for compatible Players and public venues.

🍽️

Venue-first

A venue posts when it wants a group. We look for compatible GMs and Player interest nearby.

When all three line up: the GM reviews the Players and venue, the venue confirms the space, and everyone moves into the Game Hub.

VENUES — HOST GAME NIGHTS

Your open table can start the game night.

Post the nights and times you want groups. Dinner, Dice & Dragons can match that opening with a GM and local Players. The GM coordinates the group; you provide the public table, food, drinks, and house rules.

Why a venue would join

  • Bring groups in on the nights you choose
  • Know the expected headcount before they arrive
  • Encourage food and drink purchases under your own policy
  • Have one GM point of contact instead of managing the whole group
  • Build repeat game nights if the arrangement works
