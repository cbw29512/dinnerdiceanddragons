HOW IT WORKS

Tell us once. DDD watches for the fit.

You should not have to chase strangers through posts, chats, and group messages.

1

Say what works for you

Game, available times, travel distance, or venue capacity.

2

DDD finds the overlap

We look for a compatible Player group, DM, venue, and schedule.

3

You decide

Accept or pass. When everyone needed accepts, your table is confirmed.

🛡️
DDD coordinates the match without becoming a messaging app.

No member directory. No exposed phone numbers. No user-to-user direct messages. Private contact information stays private.
