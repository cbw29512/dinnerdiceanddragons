PARTNER VENUES

Fill tables. Build community. Create a night people come back for.

Dinner, Dice & Dragons helps local venues host organized tabletop RPG groups during times they choose. You provide the table and hospitality. We help connect the players and Game Masters.

Venue Benefit

Recurring local traffic

Tabletop groups often stay for several hours, return regularly, and bring friends. Your venue becomes part of their gaming routine.

WHY HOST

A simple business case.

1

Choose slow periods

Offer tables only on the days and times that work for your business.

2

Bring in groups

Game Masters create tables and players reserve seats through the community.

3

Earn repeat visits

Successful groups can return weekly or monthly and become regular customers.

WHAT WE ASK

Keep the venue commitment lightweight.

Partner venues are not expected to run games, teach rules, provide books, or supervise the table.

Venue requirements

  • Provide a public, lawful, reasonably safe place to meet.
  • Choose available dates, times, table sizes, and any minimum purchase policy.
  • Clearly identify any age restrictions or alcohol service.
  • Provide accurate accessibility and venue information.
  • Honor confirmed table availability or notify the organizer promptly if something changes.
  • Follow the Dinner, Dice & Dragons Code of Conduct for venue interactions.

YOUR LISTING

Tell players exactly what to expect.

Your profile can show available gaming hours, seating capacity, accessibility, parking, noise level, food and drink options, and special offers.
