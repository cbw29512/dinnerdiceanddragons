WHY A VENUE PARTICIPATES

This should behave more like filling reservation inventory than hosting an event.

The platform’s job is to convert local RPG demand into predictable table traffic while leaving the game itself to the GM.

🪑

Control your inventory

Offer only the days, hours, tables, capacities, and recurrence you actually want to make available.

👥

Know the headcount

See the GM, confirmed Players, expected guests, recurrence, and upcoming sessions before the group arrives.

📈

Measure the traffic

Track hosted sessions, expected versus actual visits, average party size, repeat groups, and cancellations.

WHAT YOU CONTROL

The business stays in control of the space.

  • Days and hours available
  • Number of RPG tables
  • Maximum group size
  • Food/drink or minimum-purchase expectations
  • Age/alcohol and accessibility information
  • Approval, pause, reschedule, or cancellation

WHAT YOU DO NOT HAVE TO DO

The GM owns the game.

  • No staff member has to learn the RPG.
  • No staff member has to recruit Players.
  • No staff member has to GM or manage characters.
  • Normal venue behavior and safety rules still apply.
  • The Game Hub gives you one operational contact point.
