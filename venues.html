WHY A VENUE WOULD JOIN

You get the group. You do not have to run the event.

Game Masters are the point of contact. Your staff should not have to organize characters, rules, attendance, or game discussion.

👥

Bring in a group

Match your open table times with local GMs and Players actively looking for a public place to play.

🍔

Set your purchase rules

Require food or drink purchases, set age or alcohol policies, and define the house rules that make the group worthwhile for your business.

🧙

Work with one GM

The GM coordinates the Players and is your primary contact for schedule, headcount, and game-night questions.

YOU CONTROL YOUR SPACE

Choose when, how many, and under what rules.

You approve the booking. You decide what table is available. You set the capacity, food/drink expectations, age rules, parking information, accessibility details, and any other venue requirements.
