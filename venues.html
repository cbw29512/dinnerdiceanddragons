FOR RESTAURANTS, BREWERIES & COMMUNITY VENUES

You provide the table. The Game Master does the rest.

Dinner, Dice & Dragons helps local GMs bring organized groups to public venues. You choose exactly when a table is available, how many guests it can hold, and what purchase policy applies.

THE BUSINESS PITCH

It is closer to a recurring reservation than hosting an event.

We are not asking staff to learn D&D, advertise a complicated program, or supervise the game.

HOW A TABLE GETS CREATED

The GM schedule comes first.

1

GM chooses a time

Example: Tuesday at 6 PM for a four-hour game within 20 miles.

2

We find overlapping venue windows

The GM sees only nearby venues that have enough time available for the whole session.

3

GM builds the game

After choosing a viable venue slot, the GM creates the listing players will discover.

WHAT THE VENUE DOES

Very little.

  • Choose specific available table windows.
  • Set maximum group size.
  • State food/drink or minimum-purchase expectations.
  • Provide accurate accessibility and age/alcohol information.
  • Approve or decline a GM's requested slot.

WHAT THE GM DOES

The GM owns game night.

  • Creates and accurately describes the game.
  • Manages player seats and table expectations.
  • Arrives on time and follows venue rules.
  • Keeps the group inside the confirmed time window.
  • Acts as the main point of contact for the table.
