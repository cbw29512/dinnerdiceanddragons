WHAT WE ASK

A lightweight partnership.

Partner venues do not have to run games, teach rules, provide books, or supervise gameplay.

Venue requirements

  • Provide a public, lawful, reasonably safe place to meet.
  • Publish accurate address, availability, capacity, and purchase policies.
  • Clearly identify age restrictions and alcohol service.
  • Provide accurate accessibility information.
  • Honor confirmed table availability or notify organizers promptly.
  • Follow the Code of Conduct for venue interactions.
