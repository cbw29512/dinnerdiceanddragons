WHY A VENUE WOULD JOIN

You can actively ask for game-night groups.

Your venue is not just a location in a directory. Your available table is one of the three things that can start a match.

👥

Find local groups

Show nearby GMs and Players exactly when you want tabletop groups in your business.

🍔

Make the night worthwhile

Set food or drink purchase expectations, capacity, age/alcohol policy, and the house rules required for your space.

🧙

One GM point of contact

The GM coordinates the Players. Your staff works with one person for schedule, headcount, table needs, and game-night questions.

ALL THREE SIDES CAN START

Player request. GM offer. Venue opening.

Dinner, Dice & Dragons should not force every game to start the same way.

Nobody has to wait for somebody else to post first.

Players ask for games. GMs offer games. Venues offer places and times. Any one can reveal the opportunity that brings the other two in.
