KEEP IT SIMPLE

You provide the table. The GM handles the game.

Dinner, Dice & Dragons is designed to make tabletop groups easy to host without turning your staff into event organizers.

Offer only the days, hours, and number of tables that make sense for your business.

Your space, your rules.
