WHY THIS WORKS FOR RESTAURANTS

The venue controls the table window.

A restaurant does not promise unlimited space. It can offer something as specific as “Tuesday, 6–10 PM, two tables, six guests each.”

Venue stays in control

  • Chooses days and hours
  • Chooses how many tables
  • Chooses maximum group size
  • Sets food/drink or minimum-spend policy
  • Can require approval before the GM uses the slot
  • The GM runs the game and manages players
