RECOVERY, NOT PUNISHMENT

Knowing early that a Player cannot attend helps the table survive.

A core Player marking one session unavailable does not remove them from the campaign and does not create negative reputation. The platform can expose that occurrence to compatible session-only guests or a waitlist.
