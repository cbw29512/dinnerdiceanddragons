CLEAR ROLES

The GM coordinates the table. The venue controls its space. Players control their own commitment.

🧙 GM

Selects the venue, approves Player requests, manages table expectations, communicates headcount, and coordinates changes.

🍽️ Venue

Offers availability and retains the right to approve or decline use of its space. It does not select the Player roster.

🎲 Player

Requests to join, accepts the table expectations, and manages attendance or cancellations for their own seat.
