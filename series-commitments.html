EVERYONE CONTROLS THEIR PART

The DM coordinates the table. The venue controls its space. Players control their own commitment.

🧙 DM

Chooses the venue, approves the party, sets table expectations, and coordinates changes.

🍽️ Venue

Controls its own availability and whether the group can use its space. It does not choose the Player roster.

🎲 Player

Requests to join, agrees to the table expectations, and manages their own attendance or cancellation.
